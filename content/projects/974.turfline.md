## What it does

Turfline is business management for lawn care and landscaping companies: customers and properties, estimates with online approval, recurring service agreements, scheduling and dispatch, a mobile crew app (offline, English/Spanish, location check), invoicing and online payments, a customer portal, time tracking and job costing, reports, and integrations with QuickBooks, Gusto, HubSpot, GoHighLevel and Mailchimp.

## How it's built

- **Recurrence and routing as pure code.** The recurrence engine (occurrence dates, seasons, holidays, holds), route ordering (nearest neighbour plus 2-opt over OSRM or Mapbox drive times), pricing, payroll (weekly, daily and seventh-day overtime) and lawn-area measurement of drawn boundaries are unit-tested TypeScript shared by the server and the browser.
- **Tenant isolation and audit.** Automatic tenant scoping, encrypted gate codes revealed only by permission (and audited), and a hash-chained audit log with an integrity check in the UI.
- **Operations.** Two web pods and a worker on the Raspberry Pi workers, Prometheus metrics, Loki logs, scheduled backups with restore tests, and deploy-on-push from Gitea.

## Status

Running on the homelab cluster with fictional demo data. Its STATUS document reports 177 unit tests, 98 integration tests on real Postgres and a 38-check HTTP smoke test against a production build.
