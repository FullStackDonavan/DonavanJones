## What it does

TrueCrew is business software for painting contractors (interior, exterior, cabinets, decks): customers and properties, a measurement takeoff and bid builder, online approval with deposits, scheduling of multi-day jobs, colour schedules in an offline mobile crew app, lead-safe records for pre-1978 homes, invoicing and online payments, a customer portal, time tracking and job costing, reports, and integrations with QuickBooks, Gusto, HubSpot, GoHighLevel and Mailchimp.

## How it's built

- **Shared, tested business rules.** Pricing, recurrence, scheduling conflicts, the job state machine, route ordering and the takeoff engine are plain TypeScript in `shared/`, used by both the server and the browser.
- **Tenant isolation by default.** A tenant-scoped data layer turns cross-tenant reads into 404s; this is covered by unit, integration and HTTP tests.
- **Security.** Field encryption for sensitive values, a hash-chained append-only audit log, CSRF origin checks, rate limits, upload sniffing with ClamAV scanning, and optional MFA.
- **Operations.** Two web pods and a worker on the Raspberry Pi workers, Prometheus metrics, logs to Loki, and a Gitea pipeline that deploys every push to `main`. Platform plumbing (sessions, tenant scoping, audit chain, outbox, storage, backups, CI) is adapted from the [TPA Platform](/projects/tpa-platform).
