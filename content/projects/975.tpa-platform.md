## What it does

The TPA Platform administers self-funded and level-funded health plans for third-party administrators: employers, members, dependents and beneficiaries; versioned plans and a plan design library; effective-dated enrollment; claims intake, repricing, adjudication, EOBs, appeals and payment runs; list-bill invoicing, broker commissions, stop-loss and claims-fund settlement; COBRA and ACA reporting data; and member, employer, broker and provider portals.

## How it's built

- **Domain modules.** Identity (sessions, API keys, TOTP MFA, RBAC with row-level scope), tenancy, audit, members, plans, eligibility, enrollment, claims, appeals, COBRA, billing and more live in `server/domain`; the REST API in `server/api/v1` is a thin layer over them.
- **Explainable rules.** The eligibility engine and the claims rules engine are pure, versioned and return their reasons, so every decision can be shown to a reviewer.
- **EDI.** 834 enrollment in with 999 acknowledgements, 837P/837I claims, 835 remittances and real-time and batch 270/271 eligibility, with partner connections that switch between mock and live.
- **Security.** Encrypted, versioned documents with retention and legal hold, SSN encryption, a hash-chained audit log, and MFA required for TPA staff in production. The repository's compliance document lists what remains before loading real PHI.

Runs end to end on fictional demo data. Per its build status, what remains before real clients is mostly business, partners and hosting (tracked in the launch checklist). The homelab deployment on NodePort 30380 was not responding when this page was last updated.
